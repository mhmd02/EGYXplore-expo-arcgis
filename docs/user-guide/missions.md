# Missions

Missions are photo-based challenges. Successful completion updates your mission progress and points according to the service response.

## Browse Missions

1. Open **Missions**.
2. Use the available filters if you want to narrow the list by type.
3. Tap **Go** or a mission card to open its details.

Completed missions are removed from the available list. Open the completed-missions history from the mission header to review completed mission summaries.

## Mission Details

The detail screen shows the mission type, points, title, description, photo progress, and available actions.

## Take Mission Photos

1. Tap **Take Photo**.
2. Allow camera access if requested.
3. Take a photo and repeat until you have at least three photos.

The current mission flow uses the camera. It does not offer gallery selection for mission verification. If the camera is cancelled, no photo is added.

New thumbnails remain pending until verification. A pass or fail indicator appears after the service checks the photos.

## Verify Photos

1. Add the required three photos.
2. Tap **Verify Photos**.
3. Wait while the verification request is processed.

If verification fails, tap the red failure control on a failed thumbnail to retake that photo. All required photos must pass before the completion action becomes available.

## Complete the Mission

After verification succeeds, tap **Completed Mission**. The app submits the verification result and updates your mission progress after the completion request succeeds. A success message shows the points associated with the mission and offers **Back to Missions** or **View Rewards**.

## After Completion

The completed mission no longer appears in the available mission list. Use the completed-missions history to review it. The detail page can show **Mission Completed!** immediately after a successful completion flow.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- If camera permission is denied, enable it in device settings and try again.
- If a photo fails, retake the failed thumbnail rather than starting over.
- Verification and completion errors appear on the detail screen; your photos are not automatically cleared.
- A successful mission completion is required before points are added.

See [Rewards](./rewards.md) to learn how points can be used.
