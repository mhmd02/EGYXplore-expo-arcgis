# Troubleshooting

## App Stays on Loading

- Check your internet connection.
- Close and reopen the app.
- If the account session is no longer accepted, sign out and sign in again.

## Cannot Register or Sign In

- Read the validation message under each field.
- Registration passwords require at least 12 characters and a lowercase letter.
- Confirm both password fields match.
- Check the email and password used for login.
- If you see **Session expired**, sign in again.

## Cannot See Map Markers

- Wait for the map to finish loading.
- Make sure **Landmarks** or **Branches** is enabled.
- Check your internet connection because markers come from the map layer service.
- If the app reports missing map configuration or a map error, retry later or contact the team.

## Location Button Does Not Work

- Allow location access for the app.
- Turn on device Location Services/GPS.
- Wait for the app to obtain a GPS fix.
- If the app directs you to device settings, enable location there and reopen Explore.

## Search Returns No Results

- Check spelling.
- Try a shorter or different name.
- Local suggestions require at least two characters.
- General place results depend on the map search service.

## Route Does Not Appear

- Confirm location permission and Location Services are enabled.
- Wait for a current position.
- Check the network connection.
- Tap **Navigate** again.
- The route service can return **No route found** or fail temporarily.

## Profile or AI Image Cannot Be Added

- Check camera or photo-library permission for the action you selected.
- If you cancelled the picker, reopen it.
- For a profile image, wait for the upload and retry if an upload error appears.
- For AI attachments, remove and re-add an image before sending.

## Mission Verification Fails

1. Take at least three photos with the camera.
2. Tap **Verify Photos**.
3. Retake failed thumbnails using the red failure control.
4. Verify again.
5. Tap **Completed Mission** only after verification succeeds.

Use clear, well-lit photos matching the mission description. A service or connection error can also prevent verification.

## Cannot Redeem a Reward

- Wait for points and rewards to finish loading.
- Confirm you have enough points.
- Redeemed rewards are moved to voucher history.
- Read the inline error and retry later if redemption fails.
- Voucher sharing is not currently implemented.

## AI Assistant Does Not Respond

- Check your connection.
- If sending fails, manually resend the question because there is no retry button.
- Check camera, photo, or microphone permissions for media.
- If history is empty, use **Start New Chat**.
- If loading a stored session fails, reopen history and try again.
- If you see **Session expired**, sign in again.

## Missions or Rewards Look Out of Date

- Keep the app connected and reopen the tab.
- Close and reopen the app to request content again.
- The list screens do not currently provide pull-to-refresh.
- Indicator switches control dots only; they do not control content updates.

## Trip Action Fails

- Check your connection.
- Read title, budget, companions, and date validation messages.
- Keep at least one destination in a saved trip before saving itinerary changes.
- Use **Try again** on the saved-trip list where available.
- Pull down to refresh the saved-trip list.

## Contact the Team

If the problem continues, open **Account** → **Help** and use the displayed support contact. For an emergency, use the listed emergency hotline appropriate to your situation.
