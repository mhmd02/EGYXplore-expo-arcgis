# Profile & Settings

## Account Overview

Open **Account** to view your profile picture, name, email, level, XP, points, journey statistics, streak, and featured badge. New or incomplete profiles can show default values such as level 1 or zero statistics.

Account also provides:

- **Personal Information**
- **Settings**
- **My Vouchers**
- **Help**
- **Log Out**

## Change Your Profile Picture

From the Account profile card:

1. Tap the avatar.
2. Allow photo-library access if requested.
3. Select and crop a square image.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.

The Account flow uses the gallery. Camera or gallery choice is available during onboarding. If upload fails, the app shows an alert and keeps the existing profile information.

## Personal Information

1. Open **Account** → **Personal Information**.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Change first name, last name, country, or interests.
4. Tap **Save Changes**, or tap **Cancel** to stop editing.

Phone and email are read-only in this build and are not sent by the profile update action.

## My Vouchers

Open **My Vouchers** from Account to view redeemed rewards and available voucher codes. Voucher sharing is not currently implemented.

## Settings

Open the gear action in Account. The current Settings screen includes:

- Light or dark theme
- Language selection
- Currency selection
- Mission indicator switch
- Reward indicator switch
- Emergency contact name and phone
- **Save Changes**

These controls are currently in-session settings. **Save Changes** shows a confirmation alert, but language, currency, emergency contact, theme, and indicator preferences are not reliably persisted after restarting the app. Language and currency selections also do not currently translate or reformat the whole app.

The mission and reward switches only control whether small in-app dots are shown. They do not disable content updates or control operating-system push notifications.

## Help

Tap **Help** from Account to open the support modal. It displays the configured support email and emergency hotline numbers. Tapping a hotline uses the phone's call action when supported.

## Sign Out

Tap **Log Out** in Account and confirm. The app removes the local session and returns to the initial screen.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- Enable photo-library access if the avatar picker cannot open.
- Retry a failed profile upload or profile save while connected.
- Remember that current Settings selections can reset after an app restart.
