# Rewards

Rewards use the points available in your account. The actual reward information and voucher result come from the application's services.

## Browse Rewards

1. Open **Rewards**.
2. Review your points balance.
3. Use the available filters if needed.
4. Tap an available reward when it is enabled.

The main list normally shows available rewards as **Redeem** or **Locked**. Rewards already redeemed are shown in redeemed-reward history rather than as normal available cards.

## Check Your Points

Your current balance appears in the points indicator at the top of the Rewards screen. The balance may be unavailable while content is loading or if the service request fails.

## Redeem a Reward

1. Open an available reward.
2. Review its cost and the points summary.
3. Tap **Confirm Redemption**.
4. Wait for the request to finish.

If successful, a popup shows **Reward Redeemed!**, the reward name, and a voucher code. The balance is updated from the redemption response in the progress state. The popup also shows an informational email-delivery note in the current build; do not treat that message as proof of actual email delivery.

The visible **Share Voucher** control is present, but sharing is not currently implemented. Use the displayed code as permitted by the project service.

## Insufficient Points and Already Redeemed Rewards

Rewards that cost more than your balance are locked in the normal list. Already redeemed rewards are unavailable in the normal list. The detail screen also contains messages for insufficient points or an already redeemed reward if that route is opened directly.

## View Redeemed Rewards

Open the redeemed-reward history from the Rewards header or Account → **My Vouchers**. The history can show the reward title, description, and voucher code. It also has loading, error, and empty states.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- Wait for Rewards to finish loading before trying to redeem.
- Check your balance and verify the reward has not already been redeemed.
- If redemption fails, read the message on the detail page and retry later.
- If the reward list or history fails, check your connection and reopen or retry the screen.
