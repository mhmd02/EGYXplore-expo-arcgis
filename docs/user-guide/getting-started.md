# Getting Started

## Open the App

Open EGYXplore. The first screen shows the `EGYXPLORE` name with **Sign in** and **Sign up** options.

## Create an Account

1. Tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter your first name, last name, email, password, confirmation password, phone, and country.
3. Use a valid email address.
4. Use a password with at least 12 characters and at least one lowercase letter.
5. Enter a phone number with at least 11 characters and a country value with at least 3 characters.
6. Make sure the password and confirmation password match.
7. Tap **Register**.

After registration succeeds, the app opens the optional onboarding steps.

## Sign In

1. Tap **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap **Log in**.

After a successful login, you are taken to **Explore**. If a valid session is already stored, the app can open Explore automatically.

## Permissions

The app may ask for:

- **Location:** Shows your position and supports routes. Location is not required to browse the map.
- **Camera:** Takes profile or mission photos, depending on the flow.
- **Photos:** Selects profile photos or images for the AI assistant.
- **Microphone:** Records an AI assistant voice message.

You can deny a permission, but the related feature may not work. Location can open device settings when the system no longer allows another request. Other features may show an alert and require you to change the permission manually in device settings.

## Move Around the App

The floating tab bar contains:

1. **Explore**
2. **Sanctuaries**
3. **Missions**
4. **Rewards**
5. **Account**

Tap a tab to switch sections. A red number on the Sanctuaries tab shows destinations currently in your temporary itinerary. Small dots on Missions or Rewards indicate newly added content received while the app is connected.

## What You Should See

After login, Explore opens with a map initially centered on Cairo. The map and its content may take time to load.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- Check the email, password, and validation messages if registration or login fails.
- Check your internet connection if content does not load.
- See [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md) for feature-specific problems.
