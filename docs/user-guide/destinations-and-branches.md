# Destinations & Branches

## Destinations

Destinations are places provided by the app's destination data. The available fields differ by place. A destination may include a name, governorate, category, description, rating, visitor count, opening hours, prices, booking link, and images. If a value is missing, the detail page shows an unavailable-value message instead of inventing information.

## Branches

Branches are map features with a name, address, and contact number when those values are available. Missing values can appear as `Unknown Address` or `No Phone Provided`.

## Destination Popup

Tap a destination marker in Explore to see its name, governorate, category, and ticket price. From the popup you can:

- Tap **Details** to open the destination page.
- Tap **Navigate** to request a route from your current location.
- Tap **Add** to add or remove the destination from your temporary itinerary.

## Branch Popup

Tap a branch marker to see its name, address, and phone information. Tap **Navigate** to request a route. Branches do not open the destination detail page or add to an itinerary.

## Destination Details

Open details from a map popup or tap **Explore** on a destination card in **Sanctuaries**. Depending on the data available, the page may show:

- A photo gallery. A gallery counter appears only when there is more than one image.
- Destination name, status, rating, and visitor count when supplied.
- An **Add to Itinerary** action and the current itinerary count when there are items.
- Opening hours and entry-fee information.
- An **About** description.
- A booking link when one is available. It opens through your device's link handler.

The page also has loading, unavailable-destination, missing-image, and service-error states.

## What You Can Do From a Popup

**Navigate** and **Add** stay on the map. **Details** takes you to a separate destination page. For trip planning, see [Trips & Itineraries](./trips-and-itineraries.md).
