# AI Travel Assistant

The AI assistant can respond to tourism and planning questions when the assistant service is available. Treat its suggestions as guidance and verify important travel information independently.

## Open the Assistant

1. Open **Sanctuaries**.
2. Tap the **AI** action in the header.

If there is no history, the screen shows a welcome message and **No chat history found** when the history view is opened.

## Send a Message

1. Type a question or request.
2. Optionally attach images or a voice note.
3. Tap the send button.

The send action can include text, images, or audio. Images and audio do not require a non-empty text message. Your message appears on the right and the assistant response appears on the left when a response is received.

## Attach Images

1. Tap **+**.
2. Choose **Take Photo** or **Choose from Gallery**.
3. Select one or more images.
4. Remove an attachment with its close control if needed.
5. Send the message.

Camera or photo-library access may be required. If permission is denied, change it in device settings before trying again.

## Record Audio

1. Tap the microphone button.
2. Allow microphone access if requested.
3. Tap the microphone again to stop.
4. Remove the voice-note attachment with its close control if you want to record again.
5. Send the message.

The microphone turns red while recording. Permission denial or recording failure shows an alert. The current interface provides audio playback for audio attached to messages; assistant responses are not guaranteed to include audio playback.

## Chat History

1. Open the history view with the clock action.
2. Select a previous session to load its stored messages.
3. Tap **Start New Chat** to clear the current conversation view and begin a new current conversation.

Starting a new chat does not delete old sessions from the service. If history loading fails, the current screen may only log the failure; try opening the history again.

## Questions to Try

You can ask about Egyptian history, a destination, cultural sites, or trip planning. These are examples, not guarantees of a particular answer. Be specific and include relevant dates, locations, interests, or an image.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- Check your connection if a response does not arrive.
- If a send fails, the app displays the error as a message; there is no dedicated retry button, so you may need to resend it manually.
- If you see **Session expired**, the app signs you out and clears the current account session. Sign in again.
- If media cannot be attached, check camera, photo, or microphone permission and remove/re-add the attachment.
